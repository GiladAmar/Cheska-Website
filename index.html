About

My name is Fran Amar and I am a Counselling Psychologist registered with the Health Professions Council of South Africa (HPCSA) — PS 0156052.

I offer individual psychotherapy for adults navigating mild to moderate mental health issues, personal development and challenging life events and stressors.

Areas of interest

I provide individual psychotherapy for adults experiencing a wide range of personal concerns. My specific areas of interest include:

  • Depression
  • Anxiety
  • ADHD
  • Self-harm
  • Substance abuse
  • Eating disorders and disordered eating
  • Adjustment to chronic illness diagnoses
  • Affirming care for the LGBTQIA+ community
  • Personal growth and exploration
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Personality disorders
